# UK Legal Research

## Tagline
Case law, legislation, Hansard, bills, votes, committees, and HMRC — one MCP server.

## Description
UK Legal Research gives AI agents structured access to UK legal and parliamentary sources. Search case law from TNA Find Case Law, look up Acts of Parliament and Statutory Instruments, search Hansard debates, track parliamentary bills and voting records, query select committee evidence, parse and resolve legal citations (OSCOLA), look up HMRC VAT rates and Making Tax Digital status, and search HMRC guidance. Eight namespaced modules covering the full UK legal research stack. No API key required.

## Setup Requirements
No API keys or environment variables required. All data is sourced from official UK government and parliamentary APIs.

## Category
Education & Research

## Features
- Search UK court judgments from TNA Find Case Law
- Read judgment text by paragraph via structured resources
- Search and retrieve Acts of Parliament and Statutory Instruments
- Search Hansard debates and parliamentary proceedings
- Find and profile MPs and Lords, including declared interests
- Track UK parliamentary bills, stages, and sponsors
- Search Commons and Lords division records (voting records)
- Search select committee membership and evidence submissions
- Parse and resolve OSCOLA legal citations from free text
- Look up UK VAT rates and Making Tax Digital (MTD) status
- Search HMRC guidance documents
- Assess parliamentary reception of a policy topic
- No API key required

## Getting Started
- "Find recent Supreme Court cases on landlord and tenant disputes"
- "What does Section 3 of the Housing Act 1988 say?"
- "Search Hansard for debates about AI regulation in 2024"
- "How did MPs vote on the Online Safety Act?"
- "Parse these citations and resolve them to legislation.gov.uk"
- "What is the VAT rate for energy-saving materials?"
- "Find evidence submitted to the Housing Select Committee on leasehold reform"
- Tool: case_law_search — Search UK court judgments
- Tool: legislation_search — Find Acts and Statutory Instruments
- Tool: parliament_search_hansard — Search Hansard debates
- Tool: bills_search_bills — Search parliamentary bills
- Tool: votes_search_divisions — Search Commons/Lords division records
- Tool: citations_parse — Parse OSCOLA legal citations from text
- Tool: hmrc_get_vat_rate — Look up UK VAT rates by category
